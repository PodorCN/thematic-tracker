## VERDICT

Software was **mispriced on AI extinction fears, then re-rated on AI revenue proof**. The three recovery names were all cut in half or worse (CRM -59%, NOW -65%, WDAY -60%) and have bounced **70%+ off the April bottom**, yet still sit **30–40% below prior highs** — the rebound is real but incomplete. What changed: Q2 earnings showed AI as **incremental revenue** (CRM Data Cloud + AI ARR +120% to $1.2B, 12,500 Agentforce deals), not just a seat-count killer. The already-recovered cohort (SNOW at new highs, DDOG/MDB close behind) proves the re-rating template. The laggards (ADBE -67% peak-to-trough, still -55% off highs; INTU -68%) are either the next rotation or genuine value traps — **Adobe's AI ARR triples while its multiple compresses to ~9x forward, the widest long/short debate in software.**

The bear case never died, it just got outvoted: if AI features compress price per user faster than they expand user counts, revenue stalls while multiples stay rich. **Track NRR and AI-SKU attach every quarter — that is the theme in two numbers.**

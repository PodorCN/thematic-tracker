## VERDICT

A full year that **round-tripped to zero while QQQ gained 27%** — that 30pp gap is both the scar and the opportunity. Software spent Sep 2025–Apr 2026 being repriced from "durable growers" to "melting ice cubes" (first tremor Sep 2025, first rout Nov, January flush, February SaaSpocalypse, April capitulation), then **+40% off the April bottom once Q2 earnings proved AI is incremental revenue, not just a seat-count killer** (CRM Data Cloud + AI ARR +120% to $1.2B, 12,500 Agentforce deals). The recovery cohort (CRM/NOW/WDAY, all -60% at the lows) still sits **22–40% below prior highs**; the already-recovered (SNOW at new highs) prove the template. The laggards (ADBE -67% peak-to-trough at ~9x forward; INTU -68%) are either the next rotation or genuine value traps — **the widest long/short debate left in software.**

## CATALYSTS

### Nov–Dec 2026 (est.) | hot | Q3 earnings: CRM / NOW / WDAY must repeat the trick
The whole rebound thesis rests on one quarter of AI ARR proof. Watch: Agentforce paid-deal conversion, ServiceNow AI ARR, Workday subscription guide direction. A second consecutive AI beat closes the gap to highs; any guide-down re-opens the extinction debate instantly.

### 2026-09-10 | Earnings | Adobe Q3: laggard confirms or breaks
Next earnings per market data. Adobe's AI-first ARR tripled to $500M+ yet the stock sits 55% off highs at ~9x forward. A guide-up with organic ARR re-acceleration rotates the laggard bid; another "back-half" deferral confirms the value-trap case.

### Late Oct 2026 | AI Capex | Hyperscaler earnings + MSFT Copilot commentary
AI spend durability is the oxygen for this theme. Any cut to 2027 capex guides, or evidence enterprises are consolidating vendors instead of expanding seats + AI SKUs, hits software multiples first.

### Standing Risk | Repricing | Per-seat erosion without user expansion
The bear case never died, it just got outvoted: if AI features compress price per user faster than they expand user counts, revenue stalls while multiples stay rich. **Track NRR and AI-SKU attach every quarter — that is the theme in two numbers.**
